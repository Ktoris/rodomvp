TEEN FREELANCE LESSONS
======================

7 Beginner-Friendly Gigs You Can Start Today


---

LESSON 1: SOCIAL MEDIA CONTENT CREATOR
----------------------------------------
What you'll do: Create posts, captions, and simple graphics for small businesses.

Structure:
  1. What is social media management?
  2. Picking your platform (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook)
  3. What clients actually want from you
  4. How to write a good caption
  5. Free tools: Canva for graphics, Later for scheduling
  6. How to find your first client
  7. What to charge ($5-$15 per post to start)
  8. Mini project: Create a 3-post sample for a fake business


---

LESSON 2: LOGO & GRAPHIC DESIGN
---------------------------------
What you'll do: Design simple logos, flyers, and banners using free tools.

Structure:
  1. What graphic design freelancing looks like
  2. Introduction to Canva (free and beginner-friendly)
  3. Basic design rules: colors, fonts, spacing
  4. What files to send your client (PNG, PDF)
  5. How to price your work ($10-$50 per logo)
  6. Where to sell: Fiverr, school community, local shops
  7. Mini project: Design a logo for a made-up brand


---

LESSON 3: VIDEO EDITING
------------------------
What you'll do: Edit short videos for YouTubers, TikTokers, or small businesses.

Structure:
  1. What video editing gigs look like
  2. Free editing tools: CapCut, DaVinci Resolve
  3. Basic edits: cutting clips, adding music, captions
  4. How to export videos in the right format
  5. What clients need (YouTube intros, Reels, ads)
  6. How to price ($15-$50 per video to start)
  7. Mini project: Edit a 60-second video from raw clips


---

LESSON 4: COPYWRITING & BLOG WRITING
--------------------------------------
What you'll do: Write articles, product descriptions, or website text for businesses.

Structure:
  1. What copywriting is (and why businesses pay for it)
  2. Writing for the web vs. writing for school
  3. How to structure a blog post
  4. Using simple SEO: what keywords are and why they matter
  5. Tools: Google Docs, Grammarly (free)
  6. How to price ($5-$20 per article to start)
  7. Where to find work: Fiverr, Upwork, local businesses
  8. Mini project: Write a 300-word blog post on any topic


---

LESSON 5: PHOTO EDITING & RETOUCHING
--------------------------------------
What you'll do: Edit photos for individuals, families, or small business owners.

Structure:
  1. What photo editing gigs look like
  2. Free tools: Snapseed, Lightroom Mobile, Photopea
  3. Basic edits: brightness, contrast, background removal
  4. What clients ask for most (product photos, portraits)
  5. How to deliver edited files to clients
  6. How to price ($5-$20 per photo)
  7. Mini project: Edit 3 photos using before/after comparisons


---

LESSON 6: DATA ENTRY & ONLINE RESEARCH
----------------------------------------
What you'll do: Help businesses organize information, build spreadsheets, or research topics online.

Structure:
  1. What data entry and research gigs are
  2. Tools you need: Google Sheets, Microsoft Excel basics
  3. How to organize data neatly and accurately
  4. How to do online research efficiently
  5. Avoiding mistakes (accuracy is everything)
  6. How to price ($8-$15 per hour)
  7. Where to find work: Fiverr, Upwork, local contacts
  8. Mini project: Build a sample spreadsheet from a list of fake data


---

LESSON 7: TUTORING & TEACHING ONLINE
--------------------------------------
What you'll do: Teach other students subjects you're good at over video call.

Structure:
  1. What online tutoring looks like as a teen
  2. Picking your subject (math, science, English, coding, etc.)
  3. How to run a tutoring session on Zoom or Google Meet
  4. Making a simple lesson plan
  5. How to explain things clearly
  6. How to price ($10-$25 per hour)
  7. Where to find students: school community, Facebook groups, Wyzant
  8. Mini project: Plan and run a 15-minute mock tutoring session


---

GENERAL LESSON FORMAT (used for all 7 lessons)
------------------------------------------------
Each lesson follows this simple flow:

  [ INTRO ]       - What the gig is and who hires for it
  [ SKILLS ]      - What you need to learn
  [ TOOLS ]       - Free tools to get started
  [ PRICING ]     - What to charge as a beginner
  [ WHERE TO WORK ] - Platforms or places to find clients
  [ MINI PROJECT ]  - A small hands-on task to build your portfolio
  [ QUIZ ]        - 3-5 simple questions to check understanding
